02 How we work

A pilot is a step, not a result.

Many AI initiatives stall between a promising demo and a system people rely on. Four stages close that gap, with one senior team accountable throughout.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We start with the work, not the model: where AI can make a measurable difference, and what will be measured before anything is built.

    You get A ranked set of opportunities, a baseline, and a clear view of what is not worth doing.

  2. 02

    Build the pilot

    A small senior team builds against your real data, with real users. Regular working demos, typically weekly, keep decisions on evidence.

    You get A working system in the hands of real users, measured against the baseline.

  3. 03

    Ship to production

    Integration, security review, evaluation, monitoring and a staged rollout, against production criteria agreed with you. If the evidence does not support deployment, we stop or revise the work.

    You get A system running in everyday work, with the controls your organisation requires.

  4. 04

    Hand over and harden

    Your engineers work in the code with us during the build, so handover is a continuation. We document, pair and train, then support the system under real load.

    You get Runbooks, evaluation suites, and a team prepared to operate and extend the system.
