01 What we do

Four practices, one goal: systems your business relies on.

We help companies turn AI into measurable improvements in everyday work. That means building the system, the foundations underneath it, and the team capability around it.

A

AI agents

Agents that take on customer conversations and internal requests. We build them with evaluation, guardrails and clear escalation paths, so their work can be measured, reviewed and audited.

  • Customer service and support
  • Operations and back-office queues
  • Internal knowledge and employee requests

B

Workflow automation

We map how work actually moves through your organisation. Then we automate the steps that are suitable for it, and route exceptions and judgement calls to your team.

  • Document intake and processing
  • Review, approval and reconciliation flows
  • Reporting and routine analysis

C

Embedded engineering

Our engineers work inside your teams, your systems and your constraints. They write production code rather than recommendations, agree delivery goals with your team, and report progress against them.

  • Working with your engineers and operators
  • Building in your environment and stack
  • Delivery goals agreed and tracked in the open

D

Data & AI platform foundations

The groundwork that makes everything else dependable: clean data paths, retrieval, evaluation, monitoring and access control. We assess vendor dependencies and design for portability where it is practical.

  • Data pipelines and retrieval
  • Evaluation and observability
  • Security, governance and model infrastructure

Where engagements tend to start

Problems with high volume, clear rules and a number that already matters to the business.

Support queues dominated by repeatable requests
Measured by Time to resolution and the share of requests resolved without escalation.
Document-heavy operations such as onboarding, claims or invoicing
Measured by Handling time per case and the rate of errors caught downstream.
Expert teams interrupted by questions the organisation has already answered
Measured by Time to a correct answer and the load taken off those teams.

02 How we work

A pilot is a step, not a result.

Many AI initiatives stall between a promising demo and a system people rely on. Our method is built around closing that gap, in four stages, with one senior team accountable throughout.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We start with the work, not the model. We sit with the people doing the job, find where AI can make a measurable difference, and agree what will be measured before anything is built.

    You get A ranked set of opportunities, a baseline, and a clear view of what is not worth doing.

  2. 02

    Build the pilot

    A small senior team builds against your real data, with real users. We share progress through regular working demos, typically weekly, so decisions rest on evidence rather than status reports.

    You get A working system in the hands of real users, measured against the baseline.

  3. 03

    Ship to production

    Integration, security review, evaluation, monitoring and a staged rollout, against production criteria agreed with you. This is where most of the engineering lives. If the evidence does not support deployment, we stop or revise the work.

    You get A system running in everyday work, with the controls your organisation requires.

  4. 04

    Hand over and harden

    Your engineers work in the code with us during the build, so handover is a continuation and not an event. We document, pair and train, then support the system while it proves itself under real load.

    You get Runbooks, evaluation suites, and a team prepared to operate and extend the system.

  • One accountable team

    A senior team stays accountable from discovery through handover.

  • Regular working demos

    Working software shown to the people who will use it, at a cadence agreed at kickoff.

  • Agreed production criteria

    We define what production means with you before we build, and judge the work against it.
